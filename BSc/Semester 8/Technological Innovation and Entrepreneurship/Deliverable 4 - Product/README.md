# **ACCESS — Product Overview**

## **Overview**

**ACCESS** is an **Accessible City & Community Engagement Support System** designed as a mobile and web platform to help individuals with disabilities navigate urban environments **easily, safely, and independently**. By combining detailed accessibility information, real-time user reports, and a municipal dashboard, ACCESS tackles real-life challenges that current navigation apps overlook.

This document presents the **product details**, key features, real-life challenges, assumptions, constraints, and links to **Figma mockups and prototypes** that demonstrate the application's functionality and user experience.

---

## **Real-Life Challenges**

ACCESS is built to address daily obstacles faced by people with mobility challenges:

- Inadequate or missing accessibility data in existing navigation apps.
- Difficulty finding routes that avoid barriers and obstacles.
- Lack of real-time updates on sudden changes (roadworks, blocked paths).
- Limited channels for collecting and sharing user feedback about accessibility issues.

---

## **Assumptions**

The development and adoption of ACCESS relies on these core assumptions:

- Users are willing to use smartphones for planning and navigating accessible routes.
- Public authorities and local businesses will collaborate to verify and maintain accurate accessibility data.
- The necessary technologies — map APIs, real-time updates, voice commands, and adaptive interfaces — are available and can be integrated smoothly.
- Revenue streams (ads, subscriptions, partnerships) will be sufficient to sustain operational and maintenance costs.

---

## **Constraints**

While developing ACCESS, the following challenges must be managed:

- Complexity of integrating crowdsourced and official data from various sources.
- Maintaining high data accuracy and ensuring information is updated frequently.
- Strict compliance with data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR).
- High development costs for designing and testing custom interfaces and accessibility features (voice commands, adjustable visuals).
- UX design complexities to meet diverse user needs across multiple types of disabilities.

---

## **Key Product Features**

ACCESS offers a robust set of user-focused and municipal tools:

- **Mobile Application (Android & iOS):**
  - Real-time accessibility maps.
  - Customizable UI/UX with voice command options.
  - Crowdsourced reporting of obstacles, barriers, and construction updates.
  - Personalized accessible route suggestions.
  - Community contributions to expand and refine accessibility data.

- **Municipality Dashboard (Web Application):**
  - Overview maps with live accessibility statistics.
  - Tools for monitoring and managing reports from citizens.
  - Forms for officials to submit updates on public construction or new accessible features.
  - Visual data insights to inform policy and urban planning.
