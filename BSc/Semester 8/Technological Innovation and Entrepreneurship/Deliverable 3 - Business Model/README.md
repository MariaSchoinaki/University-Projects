# **ACCESS — Business Model**

## **Overview**
**ACCESS** is a real-time accessibility mapping platform designed to close the gap in reliable, up-to-date information about urban pedestrian pathways. By combining **crowdsourced reports, AI-enhanced data, and municipality dashboards**, ACCESS empowers individuals with mobility challenges to navigate cities confidently, while enabling municipalities, businesses, and partners to improve infrastructure and foster inclusivity.

This document outlines the **Business Model** behind ACCESS, detailing its key stakeholders, value propositions, revenue streams, cost structure, and growth approach — ensuring sustainability and social impact.

---

## **Key Stakeholders & Customer Segments**

ACCESS targets multiple stakeholders with tailored value:

- **Primary Customers**
  - **Municipalities & Public Authorities** — Gain insights for infrastructure planning, compliance with accessibility regulations, and actionable data from citizen reports.
  - **Local Businesses & Associations** — Showcase accessibility-friendly premises and attract more customers through premium listings.
  - **Accessibility Advocacy Organizations & NPOs** — Use real-time data to drive policy change and community engagement.
  - **Smart City Technology Integrators** — Embed ACCESS data into broader smart city platforms.
  - **Universities & Research Institutions** — Analyze accessibility data for urban mobility research and inclusive design.
  - **Tourism Agencies & Accessible Travel Initiatives** — Offer verified accessible routes for travelers with mobility needs.

- **End Users**
  - People with mobility difficulties
  - Temporarily injured individuals
  - Elderly citizens
  - Parents with strollers

---

## **Value Propositions**

ACCESS provides clear, tangible benefits for each group:

- **For Citizens:** Real-time, personalized accessible routes; dynamic incident reporting; customizable UI and voice commands; increased independence and safety.
- **For Municipalities:** Data-driven planning; live dashboards with reports and alerts; improved compliance with accessibility standards; higher citizen satisfaction.
- **For Businesses:** Enhanced reputation as an inclusive destination; premium advertising options to highlight accessibility features.
- **For Partners:** Valuable integration of high-quality accessibility data into smart city solutions.

---

## **Key Activities**

To deliver its value, ACCESS focuses on:

- Continuous software development and platform maintenance
- Real-time reporting and crowdsourced data collection
- Data analysis and AI-powered enhancements
- Partnerships with municipalities, businesses, and NPOs
- Community engagement and marketing campaigns

---

## **Key Resources**

ACCESS operates using:

- Robust technological infrastructure and secure databases
- Skilled development and support teams
- Financial resources for scaling and operations
- Strong networks of community contributors and partners

---

## **Revenue Streams**

ACCESS generates sustainable revenue through:

- **Municipality Subscriptions:** Paid contracts for access to municipal dashboards, live data, and reporting tools.
- **Business Subscriptions:** Premium placement and advertising for local businesses promoting accessible spaces.
- **EU Programs & Grants:** Funding for initiatives aligned with smart cities, inclusion, and urban mobility improvements.

---

## **Cost Structure**

The main operational costs include:

- Software development and continuous maintenance
- Infrastructure hosting and data security
- Salaries for staff (developers, data analysts, community managers)
- Marketing, community partnerships, and awareness campaigns
- Legal, accounting, and compliance expenses

---

## **Channels**

ACCESS engages its market via:

- Mobile Apps (Android & iOS) for citizens
- Web App for municipalities, partners, and dashboards
- Social media and digital marketing campaigns
- Strategic partnerships with public authorities, NPOs, and smart city integrators

## **Conclusion**

The **ACCESS Business Model** ensures **financial sustainability, impactful social reach, and scalable growth**. By combining subscriptions, grants, and partnerships, ACCESS is poised to become a leading solution in the accessibility technology market — helping cities worldwide become more inclusive and navigable for everyone.
