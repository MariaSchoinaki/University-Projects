# **ACCESS — Real-Time Accessibility Mapping for Inclusive Urban Mobility**

## **Overview**

**ACCESS** (Accessible City & Community Engagement Support System) is an innovative platform designed to tackle the lack of reliable, real-time accessibility information in urban pedestrian pathways. It empowers individuals with mobility challenges — including people with disabilities, elderly citizens, parents with strollers, and temporarily injured individuals — to navigate cities **independently, safely, and confidently**.

Through **crowdsourced reports**, **AI-enhanced data**, and **partnerships with municipalities**, ACCESS bridges the accessibility gap in existing navigation solutions, supporting both everyday users and local governments committed to inclusive urban planning.

---

## **Deliverable 1 — The Problem & Solution**

- **The Problem:**  
  - Cities often lack up-to-date accessibility information.
  - 1.3 billion people worldwide face mobility challenges.
  - Existing navigation tools don’t address real-time obstacles.
  - As a result, people face daily barriers, safety risks, and social isolation.

- **The Solution:**  
  - ACCESS provides **real-time accessibility maps** with verified routes.
  - Users can report obstacles (e.g., roadworks, parked vehicles) with photos and location pins.
  - Municipal dashboards enable local governments to monitor and act on accessibility reports.
  - The result: safer, more accessible pathways and improved urban mobility for everyone.

## **Deliverable 5 — Pitch & Roadmap**

- **Key Highlights:**  
  - **User Story:** Anna, a mother with a stroller, faces unsafe routes due to obstacles not shown in Google Maps — ACCESS solves this in real-time.
  - **Market Opportunity:** ~2 million people in Greece alone could benefit; initial goal of 100,000 users with premium conversion and municipal subscriptions.
  - **Revenue Projection:** ~€113,400 in Year 1 from users and municipal contracts.
  - **Competitive Advantage:** Compared to WheelMap & WheelMate, ACCESS uniquely offers live route mapping, voice commands, dynamic alerts, and municipal tools.

- **Roadmap:**  
  - **May 2025:** Finalize pitch and begin implementation.
  - **August 2025:** Complete MVP.
  - **November 2025:** Start promotion and outreach.
  - **December 2025:** Add features and begin product maintenance.
  - **April 2026:** First revenue streams confirm commercial viability.
