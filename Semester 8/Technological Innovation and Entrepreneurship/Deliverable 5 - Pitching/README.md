# **ACCESS — Pitch Overview**

## **Team & Project**

**ACCESS**  
Accessible City & Community Engagement Support System

**Supervising Professor:** Α. Καραγιαννάκη  
Department of Informatics, Athens University of Economics and Business (AUEB)

---

## **The Problem**

🚫 **1.3 billion people worldwide face mobility challenges.**  
🚫 Cities are not designed for universal accessibility.  
🚫 There is a lack of reliable, real-time data on the condition of sidewalks and public spaces.  
🚫 Individuals with disabilities, elderly citizens, parents with strollers, and people with temporary injuries encounter serious obstacles to safe, independent mobility.

---

## **Real-Life User Case**

👩‍🍼 **Anna**, a mother with a stroller, wants to walk to the park.  
🚧 She finds broken sidewalks and blocked paths, forcing her into the street and putting herself and her child at risk.  
🗺️ **Google Maps** doesn’t show real-time barriers or closed paths.  
⏳ The search for an accessible route delays her, while her child becomes restless and upset.

---

## **Market Opportunity**

📊 **Key stats:**  
- **27%** of EU residents with disabilities limit daily activities.  
- **23.1%** of Greek citizens with disabilities limit daily activities.  
- **45%** of the population has reduced mobility (including elderly, parents with strollers, temporarily injured people, and pregnant individuals).

📈 **Estimated Greek target market:** ~2 million people.  
🎯 **Year 1 goal:** 100,000 users, 1% conversion to premium (€2/month).  
🏛️ **One municipal partner per month**, generating ~€2,000 annually per municipality.  
💰 **Projected Year 1 revenue:** ~€113,400.

---

## **Our Solution**

ACCESS tackles this problem with **two core pillars**:

**✅ Solution I — Community & Municipality Collaboration**
- Public authority partnerships for live infrastructure updates.
- City dashboards and actionable reports.

**✅ Solution II — Real-Time Crowdsourced Data**
- Interactive map with live accessibility info.
- Users add & verify accessible points and routes.
- Voice commands and adaptable interfaces.
- Instant alerts for changes (roadworks, new obstacles).

---

## **Product Highlights**

**🌍 Live Monitoring Map:**  
Guides users through the city with up-to-date, accessible routes.

**📝 Reporting Tool:**  
Users can report obstacles, add photos, and share details.

**🔍 Smart Filters:**  
Users filter destinations to plan the most accessible journey.

---

## **Revenue Model**

- **Municipality subscriptions:** Sustainable, recurring contracts.
- **Business premium advertising:** Highlight accessible-friendly locations.
- **EU programs and grants:** Additional funding to expand impact.

---

## **Competitive Advantage**

Compared to competitors like **WheelMap** and **WheelMate**, ACCESS offers:  
✅ Dynamic mapping of accessible routes.  
✅ Voice command support and personalized interface.  
✅ Real-time incident reports for public authorities (via web interface).  
✅ Live notifications for changes in infrastructure.
