# **ACCESS - Market Research & Competition Analysis**

## **Overview**
This report presents a detailed **market segmentation and competition analysis** for the ACCESS platform. ACCESS provides **real-time accessibility mapping** for individuals with mobility challenges and equips **municipalities** with data to enhance infrastructure planning. The analysis explores **target market segments**, **TAM estimation**, and **competitive positioning** to define a strategic go-to-market approach.

## **Key Topics Covered**

1. **ACCESS Technology & Market Fit**
   - Real-time accessibility navigation for individuals with mobility challenges
   - Reporting system for inaccessible infrastructure
   - Dual-platform approach: mobile app (citizens) and web app (municipalities)
   - Benefits: live route updates, visual reporting, municipal dashboards

2. **Market Segmentation**
   - **Citizens with Mobility Disabilities**
   - **Municipalities & Local Governments**
   - **Local Businesses** (cafés, stores, etc.)
   - **Accessibility Advocacy Groups** (NPOs)
   - **Urban Planners & Engineers**
   - **Tourists with Accessibility Needs**
   - Each segment analyzed for urgency, willingness to change, frequency of use, and size

3. **TAM (Total Addressable Market) Estimation**
   - **Bottom-Up TAM**: Based on price per unit, budgets, and comparable solutions
     - Est. annual revenue per user: **$15,000 – $60,000**
   - **Top-Down TAM**: Based on size of global market and target beachhead
     - Est. TAM for target subsegment: **$750K – $130M**
   - **Growth & Profitability Estimates**
     - CAGR: **15%–30%**
     - EBITDA margin: **20%–50%**
     - Market share goal: **30%–50%**

4. **Key Assumptions for Market Success**
   - Speed of municipal/government adoption
   - Quality and frequency of user-generated data
   - Response from larger mapping platforms (e.g., Google Maps)

5. **Competition Analysis**
   - **Primary Competitor:** WheelMap
   - **ACCESS Advantages:**
     - Real-time path mapping
     - Dynamic incident reporting for public authorities
     - Customizable UI, voice commands
     - Municipality dashboards and live alerts
   - **Comparison Table**:
     - ACCESS outperforms in: real-time data, government integration, alerts, and accessibility design features

## **Objectives of the Report**
- **Define and prioritize target segments** based on market readiness and need
- **Evaluate the total addressable market** using bottom-up and top-down methods
- **Understand the competitive landscape** and ACCESS's unique positioning
- **Validate business potential** and alignment with social impact goals

## **Conclusion**
The analysis confirms that ACCESS has **a strong market opportunity**, especially among **municipalities, citizens with mobility challenges, and advocacy groups**. With a differentiated feature set and socially driven value, ACCESS is poised to capture a **sizable portion of the accessibility tech market**, enhancing urban mobility for all.
