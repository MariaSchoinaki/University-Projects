# **ACCESS - Real-Time Accessibility Mapping for Inclusive Urban Mobility**

## **Overview**
**ACCESS** is an innovative platform that addresses the **critical issue of missing and unreliable real-time accessibility data** in urban pedestrian pathways. Designed to empower individuals with mobility challenges, it provides digital tools and mapping features that promote **inclusive, independent navigation** in city environments.

This project aims to **improve urban accessibility**, support **legal compliance**, and enhance **social inclusion** for people with disabilities, the elderly, and others facing mobility issues.

## **Key Topics Covered**

1. **Understanding the Accessibility Gap**
   - Mobility challenges in urban areas
   - The societal impact of inaccessible infrastructure
   - Legal obligations and compliance needs

2. **Target Users**
   - **Individuals with disabilities**
   - **Elderly citizens**
   - **Parents with strollers**
   - **Temporarily injured persons**
   - **Businesses and Local Governments**

3. **Core Features of ACCESS**
   - **Real-Time Accessibility Mapping**
   - **User-Generated Reports for Inaccessible Areas**
   - **Digital Guidance for Safe Navigation**
   - **Incident Reporting with Photo & Location Pin**
   - **Local Government Dashboards for Actionable Insights**

4. **Benefits for Stakeholders**
   - **For Citizens:** Greater independence, fewer daily challenges, reduced isolation.
   - **For Businesses:** Improved customer reach, reduced legal risks.
   - **For Local Governments:** Data-driven policy planning, better infrastructure management.

5. **Broader Social Impact**
   - **Fostering inclusive cities**
   - **Encouraging community participation**
   - **Reducing social and economic exclusion**

## **Objectives of the Project**
- **Empower individuals** with real-time, trustworthy accessibility data.
- **Enhance inclusivity** in urban planning and daily mobility.
- **Support compliance** with legal accessibility frameworks.
- **Promote awareness** of the importance of universal design and equitable access.

## **Conclusion**
**ACCESS** redefines urban mobility by focusing on inclusivity, data-driven solutions, and real-time adaptability. It brings together **technology, citizen engagement, and policy alignment** to address one of the most pressing issues faced by millions—**the right to move freely and confidently in their city**.
